How it works

From file to sign-off in four steps.

You stay in control the whole way. Redline proposes; a human accepts. Originals are never touched, and a number that doesn't tie is never quietly rewritten.

The check taxonomy

Every finding, one of five kinds.

The same categories a reviewer uses by hand — so a finding lands where a partner expects it, tagged and ranked by how much it would cost in front of a client.

Typo & grammar

  • Spelling, agreement
  • Possessives (MNOs → MNOs')
  • Doubled / dropped words
  • Wrong-language fragments

Units & format

  • European vs. English separators
  • Currency spacing
  • MHz / k / CAGR notation
  • Redundant approximations

Data

  • Figures that don't reconcile
  • Chart-vs-title mismatch
  • Wrong source names
  • Placeholders left in

Storyline

  • Weak action titles
  • Duplicate titles
  • Titles the chart contradicts
  • Horizontal-logic breaks

Layout

  • Internal notes left on slide
  • WIP / draft badges
  • Off-canvas review chatter
  • Template placeholders
